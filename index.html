Projects

SO₂ Emissions Abatement - Aramco Ecothon 2026

Led the process design of a modular electrochemical process converting industrial SO₂ emissions into green hydrogen and potassium sulphate fertiliser in a 3-day competition. The core innovation exploits SO₂ as a depolariser, reducing the electrolyser cell potential from 1.23V (water splitting) to 0.17V, dramatically reducing energy demand relative to conventional water electrolysis. The team's techno-economic model techno-economic model projected £18M NPV and 17% ROI. Won 1st place at the Aramco Ecothon, competing against 15 teams.
